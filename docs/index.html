Cases

Each case is a true category, a tuning policy and an initial state; the cohorts come from the data set selected in the sidebar. All cases share the sidebar's horizon, Monte Carlo runs and α and use the same random seeds; every patient of a case starts in its initial state. Up to 8 cases.

Averages by case

Weeks, over the Monte Carlo runs. A percentile of ≥ horizon is not reached within the horizon; a mean marked ≥ counts the runs without the event as the horizon, so the true mean is at least that.

Time-to-reach — overall survival

Time-to-reach — progression-free survival

Empirical CDF of weeks to the first Progress after Disease Control, or to Death if Disease Control is never reached, for each case; P5, P50 and P95 marked under the axis.

Real client-side simulation — the environment, belief filter, agent policies and metrics run in your browser on the fitted NSCLC matrices in data/matrices.json. The agent measures the state every step (entropy threshold 0) and knows the true cohort. See docs/README.md.

Research use only. Not validated for clinical use and not medical advice. The matrices are aggregate estimates derived from AACR Project GENIE BPC NSCLC data.
